:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: questions 

- How can you assess the characteristics of a dataset and determine the appropriate statistical analyses to undergo?
- How do you visualize different types of data to reveal important or interesting characteristics?
- How can we streamline or standardize analyses to save time and reduce error when conducting the same analysis across datasets or over time?

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: objectives

- Mapping and assessing data distribution is essential to selecting the appropriate statistical approach--histograms are helpful tools to visualize data distributions. 
- Continuous and discrete variables must be represented differently: continuous data rely on accurate scaling and styles that connect observations, whereas discrete data are best visualized with individual points or bars to illustrate the gaps between data points.
- For- and while-loops are key to accomplishing iteration while minimizing error. Functions allow us to combine iteration of tasks in a standardized way that is repeatable and shareable.
